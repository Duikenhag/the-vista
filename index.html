More than a stay. A pause, a view, a memory in the making.

Before it became a destination, The Vista began as an idea: to create a space in Wayanad that feels calm, warm, and easy to return to. Thoughtfully designed interiors, open spaces, a refreshing pool, and the calm of Wayanad come together to create a stay that feels both modern and grounded.

Here, mornings open to green views, afternoons move at their own pace, and evenings invite you to do a little less and enjoy a little more.

Pool Balcony views Modern interiors Relaxed comfort

What’s inside the apartment

  • Air-conditioned bedrooms with ceiling fans
  • Floor-to-ceiling wardrobe storage
  • Kitchenette with mini fridge and kettle
  • Cooktop, sink and stone counters
  • Dining table and chairs
  • Lounge seating and a wall-mounted TV
  • Stone-clad bathroom, counter basin, hot water
  • Private balcony with planters
  • Covered pool and lawn
  • Parking under the building

Where it is

Kuppadi, Sulthan Bathery

In the Wayanad plateau, with working paddy fields on one side and palm groves on the other. The balconies face the valley; the road runs past the boundary to the front.

What you can see from the property

From the balcony
Palms in the foreground, green slopes and scattered houses across the valley, the pool directly below.
From above
Paddy fields and palm groves running to low hills in every direction.
At the door
A three-storey building in white and green, parking underneath, trees on the approach.
